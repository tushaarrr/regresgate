# Meridian billing policy (v2026.09)

The single source of truth for the support assistant. Every factual claim the
assistant makes must be traceable to a line in this file. If a question is not
answered here, the answer is "I don't have that in our policy" plus an offer to
route to a human -- never an invention.

## Plans and price

| Plan | Price | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $19 per seat per month | monthly or annual |
| Team | $49 per seat per month | monthly or annual |
| Enterprise | custom, quoted by sales | annual only |

- Annual billing is **2 months free**: you pay for 10 months and get 12.
- Prices are in **USD, EUR or GBP** and **exclude tax**.
- Price changes come with **30 days notice** and you are **grandfathered for
  the remainder of your current term**.

## Trial

- **14 days**, **no card required**, with **Team** features.
- The trial is not extendable.

## Refunds and cancellation

- **Full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase.**
- **After 30 days there are no refunds.** Unused time is credited instead.
- Cancel yourself at **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan**. Cancelling stops
  future charges; it **does not refund past charges**.
- The assistant **cannot issue a refund, credit or discount**. It explains the
  policy and routes to a human.

## Seats

- Seats added are **prorated and charged immediately**.
- Seats removed **credit the unused time to your next invoice**.

## Invoices and tax

- Past invoices: **Billing > Invoice history**.
- Add a VAT number in **Billing Details**. It appears on **future invoices
  only** and is **never applied retroactively**.

## Payment methods

- **Card**: all plans.
- **ACH**: US customers, **Team and Enterprise** only.
- **Wire**: **Enterprise only, annual only**.

## Failed payment

- **3 retries over 7 days** (day 1, day 3, day 7).
- After the third failure the account becomes **read-only**.
- Data is **retained 60 days** while read-only, then deleted.

## Data

- Export at **Settings > Data export**, as **CSV or JSON**, on **all plans**.
- **Account deletion is permanent**; data is removed **after 30 days**.

## Access management

- **SSO / SAML: Enterprise only.**
- **SCIM provisioning** is available **once SAML is live**, so Enterprise only.

## Support response times

| Plan | First response |
|---|---|
| Starter | next business day |
| Team | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | 1 hour for sev1 |

## Discounts

- **Nonprofit 30%**, **education 50%**.
- Documentation is required, and **discounts do not stack**.
